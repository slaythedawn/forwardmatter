Where the return comes from

Odds

Method: each simulated year draws the underlying from a lognormal at the chosen return and volatility (at zero volatility it is a steady path, as in the P&L; Lilly's own is about 33%, and a volatile underlying has a median path below its average return); the core overlay from a normal at the chosen mean and spread; the reader from the record's measured moves (17 advisory meetings, optionally 52 readouts, small and mid caps), a right call earning the move and a wrong one losing it, at the chosen hit rate, calls and position size, where the position is a share of the whole book and the sleeve is the cash it is funded from; the premium seller as a scenario at +4.5 points per 2% of risk with a one-in-seven tail year; the nowcaster from four prints at 6.3% at a 60% hit rate. Strategies are independent in the draw. Fees: management on capital, performance on the excess over the underlying when positive, no high-water mark. Hypothetical, unaudited, no capital at risk.
