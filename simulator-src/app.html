Two minute recap

One book, three strategies, every number a slider.

Matter Engine runs a single book. Most of it is a held position in a company whose catalysts are dated in advance; a small sleeve trades what the FDA publishes two days before it votes; a third seat is reserved for whichever engine clears its test. The simulator lets you set each one and see what the book does.

STRATEGY 01

Platform core

Hold the company with the dated pipeline (Lilly today), lift exposure into its own event windows, cap volatility. 80% to 90% of the book. Grade A: measured on five years of daily prices.

Measured · earns the underlying plus a small overlay
STRATEGY 02

The reader

Reads the FDA's own briefing document the day it posts and calls the advisory committee vote, trading only when confident, in small and mid caps that still move after the documents are public. 10% to 20% of the book, a few trades a year, two to three days each.

Measured · 195 documents since 2008 · 65% to 71% on confident calls, base rate 57% to 68%
STRATEGY 03

Next engine

The third seat is chosen by test, not by preference: an event premium seller once the option history is bought, or a demand nowcaster once script data is in. Off by default here; switch it on to see what it would add if it clears its kill rules.

Where the return comes from

Odds

Method: each simulated year draws the holding from a lognormal at the chosen return and volatility; the core overlay from a normal at the chosen mean and spread; the reader from the record's measured moves (17 advisory meetings, optionally 52 readouts, small and mid caps), a right call earning the move and a wrong one losing it, at the chosen hit rate, calls and position size, where the position is a share of the whole book and the sleeve is the cash it is funded from; the premium seller as a scenario at +4.5 points per 2% of risk with a one-in-seven tail year; the nowcaster from four prints at 6.3% at a 60% hit rate. Strategies are independent in the draw. Fees: management on capital, performance on the excess over the holding when positive, no high-water mark. Hypothetical, unaudited, no capital at risk.
