Scenario summary

How the numbers are made

Model version 2.0, 20 October 2026, fixed seed

The simulation

  1. The holding. Each simulated year draws the holding's return around the chosen rate. At zero volatility it is a steady path, as in the P&L.
  2. Strategy one. The overlay adds a draw around its average excess with the chosen spread, on the core's share of the book.
  3. Strategy two. Each confident call draws a move from the record's small and mid cap advisory meetings, posting close to the day after the vote. A right call earns the move, a wrong one loses it, less 0.4% in costs. The position is a share of the whole book, funded from the sleeve's cash.
  4. Strategy three. Each results day draws a move around Lilly's measured 6.3%. A right view earns the swing times the move, a wrong one loses it, less 0.4%.
  5. The premium seller, when switched on, is a scenario: about 4.5 points per 2% of risk, with a loss year one time in seven.
  6. Fees come off last: management on capital, performance on the excess over the holding when positive, no high-water mark.

2,000 paths a run, from a fixed seed so the same inputs always give the same figures. The strategies draw independently. Percentiles are read across paths.

Terms used

The holding
The core company held outright, Eli Lilly. Every result is measured against it.
Above the holding
The book's compounded return a year less the holding's, in percentage points.
Confident call
A meeting where the reader's model is at least 70% sure either way. It passes on the rest.
Hit rate
The share of calls or views that turn out right.
Results day
A company's quarterly earnings release.
Swing
How far the nowcaster raises or lowers exposure into a results day, as a share of the core.
Percentile
The 10th percentile is the outcome nine in ten paths beat.
Backtest
The rules run on past prices with only the information available at each date.

Plan and record

  • Plan is the setting the business plan uses. Each input shows it beside the slider.
  • Record is what has been measured on past data. Where a strategy has no past data, its record starts with live scoring.
  • The Record so far scenario sets the reader to its measured rate and the frequency the last five years supplied, and leaves the nowcaster off until it is scored.
