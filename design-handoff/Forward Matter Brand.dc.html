Forward Matter — Brand basics
00Brand basics

Substance, ahead of time.

Forward Matter is the parent company and a software licensor. The named engines sit beneath it and keep their own identities. Everything below is the minimum kit: mark, wordmark, palette, type, and the two lines that do the positioning work.

01 — Mark and wordmark
Forward Matter
Forward Matter

Four units of matter; one has already moved forward. Two words, capitalised, always — never FM, never Forwardmatter. Minimum clear space equals one grid unit on all sides.
